Letter from the Artist

I’ve always believed that music is more than sound—it’s a bridge to memory, identity, and purpose. Raised in Ghana and shaped by the sounds of Motown and New York hip-hop, I blend heritage and creativity in everything I make. From dusty vinyl samples to late-night writing sessions, this site is a tribute to the things that inspire me most.

— Trevor-James “2Grand” Otoo
